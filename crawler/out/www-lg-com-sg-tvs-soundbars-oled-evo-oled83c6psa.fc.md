The 65-inch LG C6 OLED is an outstanding TV, with perfect blacks, great brightness and overall amazing picture quality. If you are coming from an older TV, especially a non-OLED model, the difference is absolutely night and day.

The built-in speakers are surprisingly good, especially with AI Sound Pro turned on. The HDR performance, pixel brightness, picture modes and high refresh rate make this TV feel very premium. Built-in cloud gaming also works amazingly well, almost as if you are using a console. Using a PS5 with this TV is another level, with HDR making games look bright, colourful and very immersive.

WebOS is also surprisingly good compared to other built-in TV operating systems I have used. The picture quality is stunning across everything, from live TV to Netflix, YouTube and Apple TV+.

Assembly was straightforward with two people and a Phillips head screwdriver. The slimness of the TV really stands out, as the panel is incredibly thin. The stand also looks sleek, luxurious and sits at a great height.

Overall, this TV is a true all-rounder. If you are considering it, I would definitely recommend going ahead with the purchase.
